Privacy Policy

Stop Scrolling — last updated July 2026

What Stop Scrolling does

Stop Scrolling is an iOS app with a Safari extension that overlays content on YouTube and Instagram to discourage mindless scrolling. The extension reads page content solely to identify and cover short-form video feeds, suggested posts, and similar distracting elements. The app can additionally block apps you choose using Apple's Screen Time feature.

Data we collect

None. Stop Scrolling does not collect, record, transmit, or share any data about you or your browsing activity — not your identity, not the pages you visit, not the content you see. Everything described below stays on your device.

Local storage

The extension stores your settings on your device using the browser's built-in local storage:

  • Whether blocking is active
  • Your chosen overlay background colour
  • Your chosen overlay text colour
  • Your chosen overlay message (default: "Stop Scrolling!")
  • Whether Instagram Direct Messages are allowed

These values never leave your device. They are never sent to any server.

Subscription

Blocking in Safari requires a subscription, purchased through Apple's App Store. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple; Stop Scrolling never sees your payment details, Apple ID, or billing information. Apple's own privacy policy governs the transaction. The only thing Stop Scrolling stores is whether your subscription is currently active — saved on your device in a private container shared between the app and its Safari extension, so the extension knows whether to block. This status never leaves your device.

Screen Time app blocking

If you choose to block apps, Stop Scrolling uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework. Apple provides your app selection to Stop Scrolling only as opaque tokens — the app cannot see which apps you selected, and the selection is stored only on your device. It is never transmitted anywhere.

Third-party services

Stop Scrolling uses no analytics tools, no crash reporting services, no advertising networks, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. No data is shared with any third party.

Permissions

The Safari extension requests two permissions:

  • Storage — to save your settings locally on your device.
  • Native messaging — to ask the Stop Scrolling app on your device whether your subscription is active. This communication never leaves your device.

The extension also runs a content script on youtube.com and instagram.com. This script reads and modifies page content to apply overlays. It does not transmit anything it reads.

Children

Stop Scrolling does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
